🧠 Phase 1: Core Foundations

Before touching a patient, you must master the biological building blocks.

  • Gross Anatomy: Head, neck, and neuroanatomy.
  • Biochemistry: Metabolic pathways and nutrition.
  • Microbiology: Identification of oral pathogens.
Start Flashcards

🦷 Phase 2: Dental Sciences

Connecting medical knowledge specifically to the oral cavity.

  • Tooth Morphology: Carving and identifying 32 teeth.
  • Oral Pathology: Identifying lesions and cysts.
  • Dental Materials: Chemistry of gypsum, alginate, and composite.

🛠️ Phase 3: Clinical Prep

Moving from the lab bench to the simulation clinic.

  • Operative Dentistry: Drills, burs, and cavity prep.
  • Endodontics: Root canal basics on plastic teeth.
  • Radiology: Positioning sensors and reading X-rays.

📊 Phase 4: Board Mastery

Final preparation for licensure and clinical exams.

  • High-Yield Review: Case-based questions.
  • Timed Mock Exams: Simulated board environment.
  • Ethics & Jurisprudence: Laws of dental practice.
